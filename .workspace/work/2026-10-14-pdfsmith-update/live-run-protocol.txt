Acceptance and exclusions

options and inputs on that date. It does not prove all modes, models, image
formats, quality, pricing stability or future availability. Record API and CLI
outcomes separately. Refusal, truncation, missing/incomplete results, unresolved
cleanup or uncertain completion fail the pass even if some useful text exists.
Preserve recovered content and the primary error.
